# SECP — M10.10: Engineering Intelligence Authority Acceptance

This document certifies the formal boundary separating AI advisory intelligence from canonical engineering authority.

---

## 1. Absolute Authority Boundary
Under no circumstances can the Engineering Intelligence subsystem authorize releases, resolve canonical conflicts, or establish canonical truth.

### The Immutable Authority Contract
Any trace or payload serialized or exported by the AI subsystem MUST conform to this exact JSON structure:
```json
{
  "authorityTag": "ADVISORY_EVIDENCE",
  "isCanonicalTruth": false,
  "isCanonicalRelease": false
}
```
