# SECP — M10.10: Cross-Domain Intelligence Acceptance

This document certifies that cross-domain dependency mapping and freshness validation are active and secure.

---

## 1. Domain Conflict Preservation
The AI engine is an *advisory reconciler*, not an authority. If two distinct domain databases (e.g. CAD geometry vs Metrology inspection) hold contradictory information, the AI subsystem must report `CONFLICTED` status. It cannot select one domain over the other or silently decide the correct truth.
