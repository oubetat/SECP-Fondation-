## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ROOT DEFECT

Prior to this remediation, `SimulationResult` objects generated by `SimulationEngine.runScenario` lacked explicit cryptographic bindings to:
- `cadRevisionHash`
- `canonicalGeometryHash`
- Complete simulation definitions (material, mesh, solver settings, boundary conditions, and load cases)

Furthermore, downstream consumers such as `QualityGate.authorizeAiInference` and `EngineeringAiEngine.analyzeDesignContext` accepted simulation results based on basic status checks or arbitrary `provenanceHash` strings without verifying execution registration or validating `CanonicalDerivedArtifactBinding`.

### Exact Root Defect Identified:
> "Simulation results and solver outputs existed as unauthenticated data structures decoupled from CAD revision hashes and full simulation definition manifests, allowing cross-revision replay, scenario/solver substitution, and forged result promotion at engineering consumption boundaries."
