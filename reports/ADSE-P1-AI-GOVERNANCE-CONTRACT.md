# SECP — ADSE-P1: AI Governance Contract

---

## 1. AI Restriction Axiom
**AI may propose; Engineering Core authorizes.**

## 2. Permitted AI Advisory Actions
- Suggesting requirements based on natural language intent.
- Proposing traceability links between functions and requirements.
- Identifying missing verification intents.
- Performing change impact analysis.

## 3. Prohibited AI Authority Actions
- **NO Direct Canonical Writing**: AI cannot write to SECP Core revisions or system configurations.
- **NO Baselining**: AI cannot transition a requirement to `BASELINED` status.
- **NO Configuration Lock**: AI cannot lock system configurations.

Any unauthorized AI request triggers an immediate `AI_GOVERNANCE_VIOLATION`.
