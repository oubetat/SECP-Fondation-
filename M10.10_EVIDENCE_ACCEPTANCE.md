# SECP — M10.10: Evidence Binding Acceptance

This document certifies the cryptographic and relational integrity of the TwinEvidenceGraph and AIEvidenceEnvelope bounds.

---

## 1. Deterministic Evidence Envelopes
Every piece of advice generated by the Engineering Intelligence system is packed inside a deterministic `AIEvidenceEnvelope`. These envelopes are bound directly to the active CAD model revision, preventing any attempt to replay old evidence on new models or transplant evidence across different parts.
