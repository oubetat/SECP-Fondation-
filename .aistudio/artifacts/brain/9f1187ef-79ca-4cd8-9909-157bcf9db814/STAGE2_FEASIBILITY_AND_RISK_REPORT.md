This report evaluates the implementation feasibility and architectural safety of the domain binding specifications formulated in Stage 2.

### Audit of Non-Negotiable Invariants:

1. **Zero Parallel Canonical Models**: Confirmed. PCB, Electrical, Harness, Multidomain, FEM, and CFD engines bind directly to `EngineeringObjectOS` and `EngineeringIdentity` without introducing parallel model stores.

5. **Zero Metadata-Only Evidence**: Confirmed. Attestation records require exact SHA-256 content hashes of geometry, topology, material models, boundary conditions, and solver outputs generated by `EngineeringHashChain`.
6. **Zero Manual Gate Closures**: Confirmed. All qualification gates require automated, reproducible test suite verification.
