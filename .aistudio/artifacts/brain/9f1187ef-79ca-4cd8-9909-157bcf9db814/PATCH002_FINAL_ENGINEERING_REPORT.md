### Executive Summary

`SECP-CAD-TRUTH-PATCH-002` bridges the critical gap between CAD geometric identity, CFD mesh derivation, solver execution authorization, and the Engineering Truth Thread.

In `PATCH-001`, execution authority sealing was established. However, as noted in forensic review, execution authorization alone does not prove that a CFD mesh was derived from the authorized CAD B-Rep, nor does a valid provenance seal guarantee numerical convergence (`MAX_ITERATIONS_REACHED`).

`PATCH-002` resolves both challenges:
1. **Explicit CFD Mesh Provenance (`CFDMeshProvenance`)**: Every mesh generated by `CFDAdapter.meshFromBRep()` now carries an explicit cryptographic record containing `sourceGeometryHash`, `sourceRevisionHash`, `bRepHash`, and `meshHash`.
2. **Pre-Execution Gating in Solver**: `Fvm3DNavierStokesSolver.solve()` enforces fail-closed validation matching `mesh.provenance` against the HMAC-sealed `CfdToCoreBinding`. Mismatches or missing provenance cause immediate rejection (`CAD_CFD_PROVENANCE_VIOLATION`).
3. **Strict Separation of Provenance Integrity from Numerical Qualification**:
   - **Provenance Integrity (`provenanceIntegrity`)**: Cryptographically verifies CAD revision, geometry, mesh derivation, and execution authorization.
   - **Numerical Qualification (`numericalQualification`)**: Tracks solver convergence state (`CONVERGED`, `MAX_ITERATIONS_REACHED`, `DIVERGED`), iterations, force stability, and continuity/momentum residuals.
   - **Critical Engineering Invariant**: Provenance Integrity `PASS` NEVER masks numerical non-convergence, and numerical non-convergence NEVER invalidates provenance integrity unless required by a test contract.
