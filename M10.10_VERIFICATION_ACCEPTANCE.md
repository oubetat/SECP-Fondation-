# SECP — M10.10: Independent Verification Acceptance

This document certifies that independent verification cannot be bypassed, forged, or replaced by AI self-certification.

---

## 1. Non-Self-Certification Rule
AI cannot verify its own results. Independent verification requires a completely dissimilar solver (e.g. geometric constraint solver, structural finite element solver, or human authority). 
The core invariant remains strictly preserved:
```text
FULLY_INDEPENDENT_DISSIMILAR
```
This status means that the verifier does not share underlying code, heuristics, or models with the inference engine.
