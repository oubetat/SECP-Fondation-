# SECP — M10.7: Conflict Preservation Forensic Artifact

## 1. Objective
Prove that conflicting engineering facts remain independently identifiable and are never silently collapsed by AI.

## 2. Forensic Proof: M10.7-30 (Multiple Independent Conflicts)
When CAD (10mm), FEA (12mm), and CAM (11mm) provide incompatible values for the same parameter:
- AI reports `CONFLICTED` reconciliation status.
- The `findings` array preserves each conflict pair separately:
  1. `CAD ↔ FEA`: CONFLICT
  2. `CAD ↔ CAM`: CONFLICT

**Verification Result:** Conflicting canonical facts were preserved as distinct records. No averaging or silent selection was performed.

## 3. Forensic Proof: M10.7-15 (Confidence vs Conflict)
An AI model with **0.99 Confidence** was tested against a CAD/FEA mismatch.
**Expected:** Status = CONFLICTED.
**Actual:** Status = CONFLICTED.
**Verdict:** High AI confidence is insufficient to resolve a canonical engineering conflict.
