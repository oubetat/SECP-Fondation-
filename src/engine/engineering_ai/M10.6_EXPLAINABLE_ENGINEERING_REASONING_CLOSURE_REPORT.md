This formal engineering report establishes the complete architectural and empirical closure of **M10.6: Explainable Engineering Reasoning** within the SECP CAD Engineering OS.

## 2. Existing Architecture Reused

M10.6 reuses and builds directly on the established SECP infrastructure:
- **`IndependentVerificationSerializer`**: Reused for deterministic JSON canonicalization and SHA-256 hash derivation across all reasoning components.
- **`EngineeringAIEvidenceBinder` & `AIEvidenceEnvelope` (M10.4)**: Binds reasoning traces directly to evidence envelopes.
- **`EngineeringUncertaintyEngine` & `UncertaintyAssessment` (M10.5)**: Integrates model confidence, epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty, error bounds, and sensitivity profiles into the reasoning trace.
- **`TwinEvidenceGraphEngine`**: Updates evidence node attributes and cryptographic Merkle root hash upon binding reasoning traces.

The `EngineeringReasoningTrace` object provides an auditable engineering rationale:

## 4. Structured Reasoning Steps

Reasoning is explicitly structured as sequential, typed engineering steps rather than free-form text paragraphs. Valid step roles include:
- `OBSERVATION`, `INPUT`, `ASSUMPTION`, `TRANSFORMATION`, `CALCULATED_METRIC`, `COMPARISON`, `INFERENCE`, `CONSTRAINT_CHECK`, `EVIDENCE_REFERENCE`, `VERIFICATION_REFERENCE`, `CONCLUSION`, `LIMITATION`.

Step ordering is strictly validated (`sequenceNumber` = 1, 2, 3...). Steps referencing evidence or verification must explicitly supply `evidenceRef` or `verificationRef`.

---

## 5. Canonical Deterministic Identity

Reasoning identity is derived via `computeReasoningId` from canonical model parameters, context hash, execution hash, evidence ID, claim ID, conclusion, inputs, and revision hashes:
$$\text{reasoningId} = \text{"REASON-AI-" } + \text{SHA256}(\text{CanonicalReasoningPayload})[0..24]$$

Any change in inputs, revision, evidence, uncertainty, constraints, or conclusion produces a distinct `reasoningId`. No timestamps or random seeds are used in identity derivation.

---

## 6. Evidence & Contradictions Preservation

Evidence relationships are explicitly categorized:
- `SUPPORTING`: Corroborates AI conclusion.
- `CONTRADICTING`: Disagrees or identifies physical/telemetry conflict.
- `NEUTRAL`: Contextual or unverified observation.

If any binding contains `relationship === 'CONTRADICTING'`, the `explanationStatus` is automatically set to `'CONFLICTED'`, and any attempt to manually assign `explanationStatus = 'COMPLETE'` is blocked.

---

## 7. Assumptions vs Facts Separation

- `CANONICAL_FACT`: Verified fact originating from canonical CAD/PLM/Material databases (`isCanonicalFact = true`).
- `ENGINEERING_ASSUMPTION` / `AI_INFERENCE`: Unverified engineering simplification or model assumption (`isCanonicalFact = false`).
- Attempting to declare `isCanonicalFact = true` on an engineering assumption or AI inference is blocked as `INVALID_ASSUMPTION_CATEGORY`.

---

## 8. Constraint Status Traceability

Constraints preserve their genuine state: `SATISFIED`, `VIOLATED`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_EVALUATED`.
- **Crucial Rule**: `UNKNOWN` status is **never** converted into `SATISFIED`.
- If any constraint is `UNKNOWN` or `VIOLATED`, `explanationStatus` is restricted to `'LIMITED'` or `'PARTIAL'`.

---

## 9. M10.5 Uncertainty & M10.3 Independent Verification Integration

- Incorporates `uncertaintyAssessmentId` and references model confidence, epistemic/aleatoric indices, and error bounds.
- References M10.3 `verificationId` and reports independent verifier findings without elevating explanation text into a release authority.

---

## 10. Persistence, Rehydration & Zero Identity Drift

- `serializeTrace` and `rehydrateAndValidate`:
  - Rehydrates reasoning traces from persistent storage with zero-trust recomputation of `reasoningContentHash` and `reasoningId`.
  - Detects storage tampering, context drift, or evidence ID mismatches, throwing `REASONING_REHYDRATION_ERROR`.

---

## 11. Adversarial Integrity Attack Coverage

- **A1**: Injected `isCanonicalTruth = true` $\rightarrow$ Rejected (`AUTHORITY_ESCALATION_ATTEMPT`).
- **A2**: Injected `isCanonicalRelease = true` $\rightarrow$ Rejected (`AUTHORITY_ESCALATION_ATTEMPT`).
- **A3**: Out-of-order step sequence $\rightarrow$ Rejected (`INVALID_STEP_ORDERING`).
- **A4**: `EVIDENCE_REFERENCE` step missing `evidenceRef` $\rightarrow$ Rejected (`MISSING_STEP_REFERENCE`).
- **A5**: Contradictory evidence present but explanation claims `COMPLETE` $\rightarrow$ Rejected (`CONTRADICTION_UNHANDLED`).
- **A6**: Engineering assumption claiming `isCanonicalFact = true` $\rightarrow$ Rejected (`INVALID_ASSUMPTION_CATEGORY`).
