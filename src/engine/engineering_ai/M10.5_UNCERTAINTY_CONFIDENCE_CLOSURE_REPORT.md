## 2. Architectural Changes & Key Principles

### 2.1 Core Separation Invariant
M10.5 strictly enforces the structural separation:
$$\text{Confidence} \neq \text{Verification} \neq \text{Acceptance} \neq \text{Canonical Truth} \neq \text{Release Authorization}$$

An AI model claiming $\text{confidence} = 0.999$ **never** automatically causes an engineering artifact to be `ACCEPTED`, `VERIFIED`, or `RELEASED`, nor does it set `isCanonicalRelease = true` or `isCanonicalTruth = true`.

### 2.2 New Engine Components
1. **`src/engine/engineering_ai/uncertainty/types.ts`**:
   - Canonical definitions for `UncertaintyAssessment`, `EpistemicUncertainty`, `AleatoricUncertainty`, `ConfidenceInterval`, `ErrorEstimate`, `SensitivityProfile`, `CalibrationRecord`, and `ThresholdGovernanceDecision`.
2. **`src/engine/engineering_ai/uncertainty/EngineeringUncertaintyEngine.ts`**:
   - Deterministic ID derivation (`UNCERT-AI-<hash>`).
   - Strict mathematical validation (interval ordering, error non-negativity, explicit uncalibrated states).
   - Local sensitivity and elasticity profiling.
   - Threshold governance evaluator.
   - Bidirectional binding to M10.4 `AIEvidenceEnvelope` and `TwinEvidenceGraphEngine`.
   - Zero-trust serialization and rehydration.
3. **`src/engine/engineering_ai/__tests__/M10UncertaintyConfidence.test.ts`**:
   - 19 comprehensive unit and adversarial tests.

## 4. Confidence Semantics vs Hypothesis Probability

- **Model Confidence**: Represents the model's certainty regarding its own output accuracy, derived via rigorous methods (`CONFORMAL_RESIDUAL`, `ENSEMBLE_VARIANCE`, `PROBABILISTIC_BAYESIAN`, `RESIDUAL_CALIBRATED`).
- **Probability**: Represents the estimated probability of a specific hypothesis or state (e.g. $P(\text{Stress} > \text{Yield}) = 0.012$), bound to an explicit probability distribution type (`LOG_NORMAL`, `NORMAL`, `BERNOULLI`).
- Probability and confidence are structurally separate fields and cannot be conflated.

---

## 5. Epistemic vs Aleatoric Uncertainty Separation

M10.5 prevents collapsing epistemic and aleatoric uncertainties into a single meaningless scalar:
- **Epistemic Uncertainty (Knowledge Deficit)**:
  - `trainingCoverageScore` $[0, 1]$
  - `modelInadequacyEstimate` $\ge 0$
  - `outOfDistributionScore` $[0, 1]$
  - `missingInformationFactors` (e.g. `['UNTESTED_TRANSIENT_RPM_REGIME']`)
  - `epistemicIndex` $[0, 1]$
- **Aleatoric Uncertainty (Inherent Stochasticity)**:
  - `measurementVariability` $\ge 0$
  - `manufacturingToleranceVariance` $\ge 0$
  - `operatingConditionVariance` $\ge 0$
  - `environmentalVariability` $\ge 0$
  - `aleatoricIndex` $\ge 0$

---

## 6. Confidence Calibration & Anti-Fabrication

To prevent arbitrary or self-declared confidence:
- A model cannot claim `CALIBRATED` status without specifying an explicit `method` (`CONFORMAL_PREDICTION`, `PLATT_SCALING`, `ISOTONIC_REGRESSION`, `TEMPERATURE_SCALING`) and a valid `referenceDatasetId`.
- When calibration is absent or unavailable, it must be explicitly declared as `CALIBRATION_UNAVAILABLE` or `UNCALIBRATED`.
- Claiming an active calibration method under `UNCALIBRATED` status is blocked as `FABRICATED_CALIBRATION`.

---

## 7. Local Sensitivity & Elasticity Profiling

The sensitivity analysis evaluates:
$$\text{Local Derivative} = \frac{\Delta \text{Output}}{\Delta \text{Input}}$$
$$\text{Elasticity} = \left|\frac{\% \Delta \text{Output}}{\% \Delta \text{Input}}\right|$$
- Parameters are classified as `INSENSITIVE` ($E \le 0.5$), `MODERATE` ($0.5 < E \le 1.5$), or `CRITICAL_HIGH_SENSITIVITY` ($E > 1.5$).
- The profile identifies `maxElasticity`, `dominantParameter`, and checks local stability ($\text{maxElasticity} < 2.0$).

---

## 8. Error Bounds & Intervals

- **Intervals**: Mandates $\text{lowerBound} \le \text{upperBound}$ with coverage probability $\in (0, 1)$ (e.g. 95% conformal interval).
- **Error Estimates**: Values must be non-negative ($\ge 0$).
- When error metrics are not established, they must explicitly state `metric: 'UNCERTAINTY_NOT_ESTABLISHED'` with `isEstablished: false` instead of fabricating zero error.

---

## 9. Threshold Governance

The governance engine separates three distinct thresholds:
1. **Model Confidence Threshold** (e.g. $\text{confidence} \ge 0.90$): Checks if the AI model is confident in its prediction.
2. **Engineering Acceptance Threshold** (e.g. $\text{epistemicIndex} \le 0.10$, $\text{OOD} \le 0.50$, calibrated): Independent engineering policy check. If the input is out-of-distribution, it assigns `REJECTED_OUT_OF_DISTRIBUTION` or `FLAGGED_EXCESSIVE_UNCERTAINTY` regardless of high model confidence.
3. **Release Authorization**: Always hardcoded to `releaseAuthorized: false`. Release remains solely under the authority of physical verification gates, CMM inspection, and hardware-signed certificates.
