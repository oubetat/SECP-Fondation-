## 3. Critical Questions (Q1 - Q15)

**Q1: Can Engineering AI consume multiple domains without creating parallel truth?**
Yes. Every cross-domain inference is bound to the canonical `contextHash` and upstream `dependencyGroupHash`. AI results remain `ADVISORY_EVIDENCE`.

**Q2: Can SECP identify every engineering-significant upstream dependency?**
Yes. The `CrossDomainDependency` object captures domain, revision, result, and context identities.

**Q3: Can SECP detect stale cross-domain dependencies?**
Yes. Freshness enforcement rejects any mandatory dependency that is not `CURRENT`.

**Q4: Can SECP detect dependency substitution?**
Yes. Deterministic ID re-computation catches any attempt to swap identities.

**Q5: Can SECP preserve conflicting canonical facts without collapsing them?**
Yes. The `findings` structure records each conflict pair independently.

**Q6: Can AI identify a conflict without resolving it by authority?**
Yes. AI identifies `CONFLICTED` status but is blocked from resolving it to `RECONCILED` without an authoritative verification.

**Q7: Can cross-domain inference identity change deterministically when a dependency changes?**
Yes. The `inferenceId` includes the `dependencyGroupHash`.

**Q8: Can confidence remain advisory when multiple domains disagree?**
Yes. Even 0.99 AI confidence does not override a `CONFLICTED` reconciliation status.

**Q9: Can a cross-domain finding be traced through the Truth Thread?**
Yes. Integration with `TwinEvidenceGraphEngine` enables structural traversal.

**Q10: Can cross-domain evidence survive persistence without identity drift?**
Yes. Content hash re-computation during rehydration verifies bit-for-bit integrity.

**Q11: Can AI operate safely when domains are unavailable?**
Yes. Missing dependencies result in `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` status.

**Q12: Can AI remain outside domain ownership?**
Yes. AI owns only its advisory findings; CAD/Sim/Metrology retain ownership of their facts.

**Q13: Can AI remain outside canonical revision authority?**
Yes. AI cannot create or authorize revisions.

**Q14: Can AI remain outside release authorization?**
Yes. `isCanonicalRelease` is hardcoded to `false`.

**Q15: What unresolved engineering intelligence limitations remain for M10.8?**
Adversarial Intelligence Tests (M10.8) will need to explore more complex multi-step adversarial reasoning and temporal contamination.
