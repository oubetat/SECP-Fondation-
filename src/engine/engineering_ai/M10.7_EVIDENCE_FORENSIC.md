# SECP — M10.7: Cross-Domain Evidence Binding Forensic Artifact

## 1. Objective
Ensure every cross-domain finding is traceable to an evidence envelope and recorded in the Twin Evidence Graph.

## 2. Evidence Binding Logic
The `CrossDomainIntelligenceEngine.bindToEvidenceEnvelope` method:
1. Validates that the trace `contextHash` matches the `AIEvidenceEnvelope`.
2. Injects the `CrossDomainInferenceTrace` into the node attributes of the `TwinEvidenceGraphEngine`.
3. Recomputes the node content hash to ensure cryptographic integrity.

## 3. Forensic Proof: P4 (Valid Evidence Binding)
A cross-domain finding was bound to `EV-AI-001`.
- The `TwinEvidenceGraph` root hash was updated.
- The node attributes now contain the `reconciliationStatus` and `dependencyGroupHash`.

**Verdict:** Cross-domain claims are structurally bound to the SECP evidence chain. A claim without an evidence binding is rejected by the system.
