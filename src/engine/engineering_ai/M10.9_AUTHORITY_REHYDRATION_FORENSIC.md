# SECP — M10.9: Authority Rehydration Forensic Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report validates that rehydration can never escalate the authority boundary of an AI output.

## 2. Invariants Audited

2. **Canonical Exclusions**: `isCanonicalTruth` and `isCanonicalRelease` must strictly equal `false` or `undefined` (never `true`).

## 3. Test Evidence

- **Test A04**: Proves that setting `isCanonicalTruth = true` causes immediate rehydration rejection.
- **Test A05**: Proves that setting `isCanonicalRelease = true` causes immediate rehydration rejection.
