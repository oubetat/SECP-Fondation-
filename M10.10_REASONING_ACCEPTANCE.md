# SECP — M10.10: Explainable Engineering Reasoning Acceptance

This document certifies that AI-generated reasoning traces are structured, verifiable, and cannot bypass logical validation.

---

## 1. Trace Structure & Validation Rules
Reasoning traces are structured into formal step lists. This separates **Canonical Facts** from **Engineering Assumptions** and **Model Inferences**. 
Key logical rules are enforced:
1. **No Silent State Promotion**: Unknown or un-evaluated constraints (`UNKNOWN`) must never silently resolve to `SATISFIED`.
2. **Preserve Contradictions**: Traces containing contradicting evidence must never be reported as `COMPLETE` or clean.
