ARCHITECTURE:
- Geometry: Pure math contracts established (Point, Vector, Curve, Surface).
- Topology: Boundary limits established referencing Geometry (Vertex, Edge, Face, Solid).
- B-Rep: Manifold hierarchy models preserving adjacency and metadata (Solid -> Shell -> Face -> Wire -> Edge -> Vertex).
- NURBS: Strict mathematical surfaces/curves supporting degree, control points, weights, knots.
- Modeler: Contract boundary defining kernel operations (e.g., booleanCut) returning strongly-typed KernelOperationResult with CapabilityStatus.
- History: Encapsulated FeatureGraph with inputs, topology outputs, and feature dependencies.
- Constraints: Declarative constraints referencing topology.
- Backend: CADKernelBackend adapter for Geometry, Topology, and BRep delegation.
- Numerical Policy: Central TolerancePolicy (Absolute 1e-7m, Relative 1e-5) established.
